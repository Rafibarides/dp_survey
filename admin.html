Results dashboard

Read this as a lab readout, not a beauty contest. Selection rate is the main signal. Ranking breaks ties. Familiarity is inferred from the food and phrase asides, never shown to respondents.

Overall top six

The set across every respondent. Ordered by final score (70% how often selected into the top six, 30% how strongly ranked when selected). #1 is the strongest consensus pick.

Least familiar top six

Exact ranked shortlist from the respondent with the lowest familiarity score. If several people tie for lowest, their picks are pooled. The stranger group consensus sits underneath for comparison.

Stranger group consensus

Aggregate top six from everyone scored as a stranger. Useful when more than one person tied for knowing you least.

How well do people know you?

Built from the two diagnostic asides. Bagel + your real phrases push the score up. Mushroom soup + the stiff goodbye push it down. Score 3 to 4 counts as knows you well.

Top six if they know you

Same scoring as overall, but only among respondents scored as knowing you.

Top six from strangers

Same scoring, only among respondents scored as strangers.

Biggest taste gaps

Photos where people who know you and strangers disagree most. Positive delta means the knows-you group selects it more.

Most common number one

Which photo people crown as their single favorite most often. This can differ from the overall top six if a polarizing photo is loved by a minority.

Weakest performers

Lowest final scores. Rarely shortlisted, or shortlisted but ranked weakly.

Highest polarization

Highest disagreement. People either put these high or ignore them. Low polarization means stable consensus.
